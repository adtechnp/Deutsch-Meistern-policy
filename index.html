Privacy Policy

Deutsch Meistern

Effective Date: September 12, 2026 • Last Updated: September 12, 2026
Privacy Summary: Deutsch Meistern is designed with privacy at its core. We do NOT collect, track, or share your personal data. All your learning progress is stored locally on your own device.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information: We do not collect, request, or store any personal data such as your name, email address, physical location, phone number, or device identifiers.

Local Learning Data: Your lesson progress, quiz scores, XP points, daily goal streaks, and dictionary preferences are stored exclusively on your device using local storage. This information is never transmitted to any external servers.

2. Permissions Used

Permission Purpose
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Delivers optional daily "Word of the Day" reminders. Can be disabled at any time in system settings.
VIBRATE Provides tactile haptic feedback during quiz interactions and button presses.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Allows scheduled local study notifications to resume after restarting your device.

3. Text-to-Speech (TTS) & Audio

The app uses your device's native Text-to-Speech engine to pronounce German words. We do not access your microphone, and no audio is recorded or stored.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Deutsch Meistern does not integrate third-party advertising networks, marketing trackers, or analytical tracking SDKs. The app functions 100% offline.

5. Children's Privacy

Because no personal information is collected from any user, Deutsch Meistern is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
